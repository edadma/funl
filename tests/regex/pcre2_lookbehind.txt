# The lines of lookbehind.txt where PCRE2 compiles the pattern and this engine still answers differently,
# `line<TAB>reason`. PCRE2 tries the starts a lookbehind could have, the farthest first, and matches forward
# from each; this engine matches the lookbehind backward from the position. Whether a plain lookbehind
# holds is the same either way, which alternative or repetition count a group inside it takes, which turn
# of a repeated group it keeps, a backreference to a group inside it, and an atomic group inside it are
# not. The other lines of lookbehind.txt that PCRE2 cannot answer are the 20 it refuses as unbounded, which
# the test counts and does not list. Whether the 9 below should change is open with the user.
32	backward the first alternative wins; PCRE2, starting as far back as it can, takes aa
33	lazy backward takes one a; PCRE2, starting as far back as it can, takes two
34	backward the lazy group is matched first and takes nothing; PCRE2 gives each group an a
35	backward the second group is matched first and takes the a; PCRE2 gives it to the first
36	a group keeps its last turn, which backward is the leftmost; PCRE2 keeps (2,4)
37	backward the backreference is reached before its group, which has captured nothing; PCRE2 answers (2,3)(0,1)
38	backward the group is matched first, so the backreference on its left sees it; PCRE2 finds no match
39	backward the atomic group is entered from its right end, where only ab fits; PCRE2 commits to a and finds no match
40	backward the a outside the atomic group is taken first; PCRE2's atomic run takes both a's and finds no match
